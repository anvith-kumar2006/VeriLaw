# Slide 27 — Judge Q&A

## Why Flask?

- Lightweight
- Easy REST API development
- Ideal for rapid prototyping
- Well suited for hackathons

---

## Why MySQL?

- Reliable relational database
- Strong support for foreign keys
- Easy integration with Flask

---

## Why Machine Learning instead of an LLM?

For this MVP:

- Complaint classification is a structured task.
- Traditional ML (TF-IDF + Logistic Regression) is fast, lightweight, explainable, and works offline.
- It avoids dependency on paid APIs and reduces latency.

LLMs can be added in future versions for advanced legal assistance.

---

## Why OCR?

Many users have:

- Bills
- Receipts
- Notices
- Letters

OCR reduces manual data entry and extracts useful information automatically.

---

## What makes Judiciary Flow unique?

- AI-guided complaint preparation
- Department recommendation
- OCR-based evidence extraction
- Automated complaint document generation
- Clean and simple user experience
- Designed specifically for legal complaint assistance

---

## How can it scale?

- Cloud deployment
- Government integrations
- Mobile applications
- Multilingual support
- AI-powered legal guidance
- Analytics and reporting
