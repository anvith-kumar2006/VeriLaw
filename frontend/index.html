Three Steps to a Verified Complaint

From document upload to a print-ready complaint package in minutes — no legal knowledge required.

1

Describe Your Issue

Tell us what happened in plain language. Our AI classifies your complaint type and recommends the correct government authority — Consumer Commission, Cyber Crime Portal, Labour Department, and more.

2

Upload Evidence
